Alignment & advanced toolsJigs, measurement, outline capture and low-power framing

Jig profiles

The pocket origin uses absolute machine X/Y after switch homing. Designs use the pocket's lower-left corner as canvas X0 Y0.

Select or save a jig. Home before using its machine coordinates.

Jog and measure

Home, jog to the object's top-left corner, capture A, then jog to its bottom-right corner and capture B. Each capture reads a fresh GRBL machine position.

No measurement captured.

The orange box appears in the design canvas. Select artwork there and use Fit selection inside measured area.

Capture item outline

Home, then jog the head to points around the item's perimeter. Capture each point in order. The purple outline appears on the design grid so you can place artwork over it. The laser does not detect edges automatically.

No outline captured.

This is a visual guide only. It does not become engraving G-code. Capture again after homing, changing jig, or reconnecting.

Physical low-power framing

Home and check the work area first. PWM is capped at 1% of the controller's $30 value and at S10. Low power can still mark sensitive material; test on scrap under supervision.

Framing is disabled until switch homing and controller settings are verified.
